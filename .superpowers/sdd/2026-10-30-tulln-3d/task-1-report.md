## Integration contract

All imports are ordinary TS modules with named exports. Grid cells use integer `x,y`, row-major index `y*width+x`; one cell is 2 metres. World rendering can choose its own origin but must retain these grid coordinates for commands. Heights, water depths and sills are metres; time is seconds.

- `createTerrain(width=256,height=192): Terrain` from terrain.ts. Default 49,152 cells; deterministic Float64Array `heights`, Uint8Array `river` and `obstacles`; buildings have `id,x,y,width,height,people`. Gate has `id,a,b,x,y,sill,edges?`. `a,b` are representative adjacent indices; `edges` contains the complete seven-edge span. Closed gates must visually span this channel. Buildings occupy obstacle cells. Cell heights are the exact shared render/solver terrain.
- `createGame(terrain?: Terrain): Game` from game.ts. Starts running with 900 resources; 35 houses and 140 people on default map. Terrain is immutable during gameplay; do not transfer its live buffers out of the owner worker.
- `commandGame(game, command): CommandResult` returns `{ok:boolean,reason?:string,changed?:number}`. Commands are `{type:'barrier'|'remove',points:Point[]}`, `{type:'rescue',buildingId:number}`, `{type:'gate',gateId:number,open:boolean}`, and `{type:'pause'|'resume'|'reset'}` (individual discriminated variants). `Point={x:number,y:number}` in cell coordinates. Commands mutate owned game state. A horizontal drawn segment creates north/south blocking edges; vertical creates east/west edges. Diagonals rasterize to connected Manhattan edge segments. Points must lie inside the outer border. New lines are atomic; overlap is deduplicated. Reject obstacle/gate crossings, depth >0.65m, invalid coordinates, insufficient resources.
- `stepGame(game, dtSeconds): void`. Use fixed 1/30-second worker ticks. Internally subdivides at 0.1s maximum, freezes paused states and finishes at exactly360s. No hidden wall clock or RNG. Invalid/negative dt throws. Reset reconstructs initial state with the same terrain.
- `snapshotGame(game): Snapshot` clones all mutable snapshot data. `depths` is a detached Float32Array suitable for transfer; `width,height,cellSize,time,duration,phase,resources`; barrier array (`key,a,b,x,y,height,progress,status,cost`); rescue array (`buildingId,elapsed,status`); building states (`buildingId,depth,peakDepth,damage,rescued`); gate array (`id,open`); accounting and results. Phase is running/paused/finished. Barrier status is queued/building/ready; rescue active/complete. Building damage is0..1. Snapshot mutation never changes the simulation.
- Results: `rescued,totalPeople,safePeople,threatenedPeople,damagedBuildings,meanDamage`. safePeople are unrescued residents whose home never exceeded0.1m; threatenedPeople are everyone whose home exceeded0.1m at any time, including subsequently rescued people. Therefore threatenedPeople can overlap rescued; safePeople and rescued do not overlap. Damage is depth-duration accumulated above0.1m; damagedBuildings means damage>0.05.
- Constants from game.ts: DURATION360, INITIAL_RESOURCES900, BARRIER_HEIGHT1.1, MAX_BUILD_DEPTH0.65.
- Lower-level simulation.ts: `createSimulation(terrain)`, `stepSimulation(simulation,dt,forcing=true)`, `waterVolume(simulation)`, `floodStage(time)`, `edgeKey(a,b)`. `depths` Float64, dense sillX/sillY arrays (absolute elevations, -Infinity absent). For custom solver fixtures set sillX at west cell for east edge, sillY at north cell for south edge. `sills` Map is diagnostic gameplay mirror, not the solver's authoritative input. Game commands own sill buffers; do not edit them during gameplay.

## Behavior and calibration

Two-metre cells, curved broad river, low waterfront and roads/fields, impermeable building footprints. Continuous1.55m embankment and seven-cell relief channel with2m gate sill; open by default. No isolated bypassable one-edge gate. River cells exchange water with a prescribed upstream reservoir; each exchange is explicitly accounted as inflow/outflow. Outer land boundaries are closed. Stage is0→0.03m over first45s, rises to2.15m at180s, recedes to0.05m at360s. This is a stylised local game, not a surveyed flood model, shallow-water momentum solver, or real hazard forecast.

Each adjacent flux uses hydraulic head above the maximum ground/barrier sill. All donor outflows share a positivity bound; equal/opposite fluxes conserve volume. Barriers raise edge sills and never alter terrain/water floors; build to full1.1m over2s and permit overtopping. Each edge costs2 resources. Paused lines reserve resources; resume revalidates and fully refunds rejected queued edges. Removal refunds100% queued/50% started costs. Six-second rescues run in parallel, repeated commands are idempotent, and pausing freezes their clocks.

Full360s no-action calibration: open gate7 damaged houses, meanDamage0.0984773; closed gate7 damaged houses, meanDamage0.0872147 (11.4% lower).42 residents threatened,98 never exposed; first waterfront rows threatened, back rows safe. Largest open-case house peak depth0.6743m. Volume residual open -8.85e-9m³, closed -1.54e-8m³.
