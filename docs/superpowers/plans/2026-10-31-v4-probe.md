Frequent tested commits and pushes origin/codex/wien-v4-probe. Cheapest adequate agent, upgrade only with recorded reason.

Actual geodata versus labelled fictional breach/calibration. Visible placement geometry agrees with solver. Damage cannot be farmed or repaired by demolition. UI errors/loading and keyboard focus.
